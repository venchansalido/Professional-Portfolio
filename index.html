ABOUT ME

I'm a Filipino software developer who just graduated with a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology from Western Mindanao State University. What I bring to the table isn't just code — it's a track record of delivering software that real organizations trust and depend on.

"Designated as lead developer by the client — not just an intern, but the person who built and deployed the system."

During my OJT at DOLE Regional Office IX, I replaced the entire regional office's manual Excel payroll operations with a full Laravel-based platform I designed, built, tested, and deployed — including role-based access, automated government remittances, digital payslips, and TEV approval workflows.

My capstone project, ZECURE, is a live cross-platform public safety app for Zamboanga City — real-time crime mapping, safe-route navigation, and push alerts, built with Flutter, Supabase, and PostGIS. It's deployed and public.

I pick the right tool for the problem: Laravel for enterprise backends, Flutter for mobile, VBA for field automation, PHP for rapid deployment. I write documentation, train end-users, and stay until the thing actually works.
