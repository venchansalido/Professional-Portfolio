Developer Profile: Tools of the Trade

A survey of the technologies that power Salido's most impactful work.

Laravel serves as his primary web framework — bringing MVC discipline and expressive PHP to government systems and insurance platforms alike. MySQL underpins most relational data needs, while PostgreSQL paired with PostGIS handles spatial queries in his crime-mapping work.

On the mobile front, Flutter's cross-platform capabilities allow a single codebase to reach both Android and iOS users. Firebase Cloud Messaging handles push notifications, while Supabase provides a real-time backend that complements the Flutter ecosystem without the overhead of a custom API layer.

For automation, Microsoft Excel with VBA macros remains a powerful ally — enabling sophisticated CPM scheduling, dynamic Gantt charts, and barcode-driven compliance tracking to be deployed on hardware already present in any office.

On the Record: What Makes Him Different

Colleagues and collaborators note a rare combination of traits.

In a field where specialists abound, Salido occupies an uncommon position: equally at home designing a PostgreSQL schema, writing a Flutter widget tree, scripting an Excel macro, and explaining the result to a non-technical government official.

His background in Computer Engineering — before transitioning to Information Technology — gives him a hardware intuition that most web developers lack. Troubleshooting network faults, reformatting systems, and diagnosing hardware failures are second nature.

For recruiters and project managers: he holds the Civil Service Professional eligibility, making him eligible for government IT positions, and remains open to both employment and freelance engagements.
